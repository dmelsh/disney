## What are the exact 2026 dates for the fall festival, the 2026 theme, and the park hours (especially Oct 17-31)?

### Takeaway
The 2026 fall festival is Harvest Festival presented by Humana, September 11 through October 31, 2026, with the nighttime Pumpkins In The City presented by Mercy running September 18 through October 31. The theme is "acres of crafts by day and more than 20,000 pumpkins aglow at night." Exact hours for Oct 17-31 could not be verified from the official calendar. Third-party trackers show roughly 10 am to 9 pm most days and 9:30 am to 10 pm on Fridays and Saturdays, and they conflict with one generic listing.

### Cited Findings
- Harvest Festival presented by Humana runs Sept. 11 through Oct. 31, 2026, per the park's own press release (summary: "acres of crafts by day and more than 20,000 pumpkins aglow at night"). Search tool attributed this to the official release, which it dated September 4, 2026 — [SDC press release: Harvest Festival 2026](https://www.silverdollarcity.com/press/harvest-festival-2026/) (also indexed at https://prodcms.silverdollarcity.com/press/harvest-festival-2026/); not directly fetched.
- Same dates (Sep 11 - Oct 31, 2026) appear in the park's festival listing page and in a regional tourism listing, which also notes hours of operation vary — [SDC 2026 Festivals & Events](https://prodcms.silverdollarcity.com/theme-park/festivals26/); [Visit Table Rock Lake listing](https://business.visittablerocklake.com/events/details/silver-dollar-city-harvest-festival-14340)

### Inferences
- Oct 17-31, 2026 falls in the final two weeks of the festival and the Pumpkins In The City run, so both the daytime craft program and the nighttime pumpkin lighting should be active throughout the family's window.
- Because hours are sourced only from trackers, a family should plan around a Friday/Saturday later close (about 10 pm) versus about 9 pm other days, and verify on the official calendar before booking.
- The last day of the festival is Saturday, Oct 31 (Halloween), likely a heavy day.

## What shows, concerts, parades and special entertainment run during the festival, and which suit toddlers vs. grandparents?

### Takeaway
The three official 2026 festival concerts at Echo Hollow Amphitheatre (Diamond Rio Sep 12, Aaron Tippin Sep 19, Sara Evans Sep 26) have all already happened, so no headliner concert falls in Oct 17-31. Late-October entertainment is the daily park lineup plus the nightly Pumpkin Plaza dance party and character meet-and-greets. No scare zones or haunted houses are listed, which suits grandparents and toddlers.

### Cited Findings
- Three country concerts at Echo Hollow Amphitheatre: Diamond Rio Sept 12, Aaron Tippin Sept 19, Sara Evans Sept 26, 2026, per the official release — [SDC press release](https://www.silverdollarcity.com/press/harvest-festival-2026/); Sara Evans's date confirmed in a follow-up search — [Lake.com: Sara Evans at SDC 2026](https://www.lake.com/events/sara-evans-concert-at-silver-dollar-city/) (via search summary)
- Echo Hollow shows are included with admission; blankets and low-back lawn chairs are allowed; outside food and drink are not — [Lake.com: Sara Evans at SDC 2026](https://www.lake.com/events/sara-evans-concert-at-silver-dollar-city/) (via search summary)
- Pumpkin Plaza: all-ages dance party and character meet-and-greets are in the official 2026 release. A "blacklight and bubble dance party" starting around 5:30 pm is described by third-party guides — [SDC press release](https://www.silverdollarcity.com/press/harvest-festival-2026/); [Thrillzing](https://thrillzing.com/seasonal-events/harvest-festival-2026-silver-dollar-city/); [Fora Travel guide](https://www.foratravel.com/guides/N8FEFF/family-fun-and-fall-a-weekend-at-silver-dollar-city-triplescoop-travel). The blacklight wording is documented for earlier years and not confirmed in the 2026 official text.
- Listed daily shows in one guide include Lucky the Rescue Dog, old-time country music at McHaffie's Homestead, a comedy street troupe, amphitheater music recitals and the Wild West Show — [Thrillzing](https://thrillzing.com/seasonal-events/harvest-festival-2026-silver-dollar-city/) (third-party; may be generic rather than 2026-specific)
- 2025 baseline: stage acts mentioned included the Rivertown Ramblers, the Silver Dollar Saloon and Eastern Heights — [Tripster 2025 guide](https://www.tripster.com/travelguide/event/harvest-festival/)
- No scare zones or haunted houses at Harvest Festival; costumes are not allowed for guests — [Thrillzing](https://thrillzing.com/seasonal-events/harvest-festival-2026-silver-dollar-city/); [SDC Harvest Festival page](https://www.silverdollarcity.com/theme-park/festivals/harvest-festival)
- Toddler suggestions: The Fire District at the back of the park, Elephant March model animals and FireHouse Play Place; Fire in the Hole is scarier and for older kids. Grandparents: the steam train as a rest, an afternoon break, then return at sundown for the lights — [Fora Travel guide](https://www.foratravel.com/guides/N8FEFF/family-fun-and-fall-a-weekend-at-silver-dollar-city-triplescoop-travel) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- With the concerts over, the evening anchor for this family is the Pumpkin Plaza dance party and pumpkin lights, likely suitable for the 5-, 2.5- and 1.5-year-olds, though the late hours may be hard for the youngest.
- The no-costume rule means kids should not wear Halloween costumes into the park, which matters on Oct 31.

## Which craft demonstrations and visiting craftsmen are featured, and where? What festival foods and pumpkin items are offered?

### Takeaway
Dozens of visiting craftsmen demonstrate daily (copper art, jewelry making, stained glass, glassblowing, blacksmithing), and a Cowboy Emporium in the Carousel Barn plus daily cowboy cooking demos with an afternoon chuck wagon cook-off are official features. Food coverage is mostly third-party and 2025-based: pumpkin funnel cakes, apple dumplings with pumpkin caramel, and pumpkin spice rubbed ribs. No official 2026 menu or named craftsmen list was found.

### Cited Findings

## Are there trick-or-treat events, pumpkin displays, scarecrow contests, or other kid-focused fall activities?

### Takeaway
Pumpkin displays are the main draw: more than 20,000 glowing pumpkins at night, plus the Garden of Giants with pumpkins over 1,000 pounds. Kid activities are Peter & Penelope Pumpkin meet-and-greets with professional photos, the Pumpkin Plaza dance party and Pumpkin Plaza games and crafts. I found no evidence of trick-or-treating or a scarecrow contest.

### Cited Findings
- More than 20,000 pumpkins aglow at night; Garden of Giants pumpkins weigh more than 1,000 pounds — [SDC press release 2026](https://www.silverdollarcity.com/press/harvest-festival-2026/) (via search summary)
- 2025 Garden of Giants examples: "Alexander The Greatest" (1,604 lbs) and "Duncan the Punkin" (1,586 lbs) — [Tripster 2025 guide](https://www.tripster.com/travelguide/event/harvest-festival/) (2025 baseline)
- Guests can meet Peter & Penelope Pumpkin and get professional photos — [SDC Harvest Festival page](https://www.silverdollarcity.com/theme-park/festivals/harvest-festival)
- Pumpkin Plaza has games and crafts, and pumpkin displays extend into Rivertown along with the Woodland Hike path — [Tripster](https://www.tripster.com/travelguide/event/harvest-festival/); [Thrillzing](https://thrillzing.com/seasonal-events/harvest-festival-2026-silver-dollar-city/) (third-party)
- Costumes are not allowed for guests — [SDC Harvest Festival page](https://www.silverdollarcity.com/theme-park/festivals/harvest-festival)

### Inferences
- The absence of trick-or-treating and the costume ban mean Halloween-style kid activity here is photo ops, dance and pumpkin viewing, not candy collection.

## Typical crowd levels, busiest days/times, and Branson weather in mid-late October

### Takeaway
Weekends, especially Saturdays, are the busiest and weekdays (Thursdays and Fridays late in the month) are lighter, based on forecast-style crowd calendars rather than measured attendance. Branson mid-late October is mild: highs around 70-72 F, lows from the 40s, with sources disagreeing on rainfall (about 1.3 to 3.3 inches for the month).

### Cited Findings
- Parkqueuetimes forecast October 2026 quietest days: Fri Oct 9, Wed Oct 14, Thu Oct 29, Fri Oct 30; busiest forecast Sat Oct 3; Saturdays generally heavy — [Parkqueuetimes crowd calendar](https://parkqueuetimes.com/parks/silver-dollar-city/calendar)
- Weekday crowds lighter, weekends busier, evening pumpkin lighting a major draw; these are predictions, not measured attendance — [Thrill-Data crowd calendar](https://www.thrill-data.com/trip-planning/crowd-calendar/silver-dollar-city); [Queue-Times calendar](https://queue-times.com/en-US/parks/10/calendar) (the Queue-Times page appears dated)
- Advice in one guide: take an afternoon break and return at sundown for the lights — [Fora Travel guide](https://www.foratravel.com/guides/N8FEFF/family-fun-and-fall-a-weekend-at-silver-dollar-city-triplescoop-travel)
- Weather: October average highs about 72 F, lows about 42-49 F depending on source; evenings can drop into the low 40s later in the month — [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/weather/58183/10/branson-weather-in-october); [Tripster Branson October weather](https://www.tripster.com/travelguide/weather-in-branson-mo-in-october/)
- Rain: about 3.3 inches for October in a long-term climate table versus about 1.3 inches over about 3 rainy days in a recent-years source; Wanderlog gives a 24% chance of rain on an average day — [Climate table](https://countrystudies.us/united-states/weather/missouri/branson.htm); [Weather-stats](https://weather-stats.com/usa/branson_mo/october); [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/weather/58183/10/branson-weather-in-october)
- Fall foliage often peaks around the third week of October — [Tripster Branson October weather](https://www.tripster.com/travelguide/weather-in-branson-mo-in-october/)

### Inferences
- The family's Oct 17-31 window includes Sat Oct 17, 24 and 31 (expected heaviest). Thu Oct 29 and Fri Oct 30 are the best forecast low-crowd days, though Oct 30 is also a late-closing Friday and right before Halloween weekend.
- Dress in layers: warm afternoons (around 70 F) but chilly evenings for the pumpkin lights, especially for the 1.5- and 2.5-year-olds; bring a light rain layer.

### Gaps
- No measured attendance data or official statement on crowd levels.
- Crowd forecasts from the calendars were found only for early October dates in the summaries; specific forecasts for Oct 17-28 were not captured.
- No day-specific weather forecast for Oct 17-31, 2026.
