## 1. Official height requirements and accompanying-rider rules, ride by ride

### Takeaway
No ride was confirmed to have "no minimum height" for a 1.5-year-old (Emma). Every ride I could source has a 36-inch floor, or lets guests under 36 inches ride only with a "supervising companion". Defined by the park as a responsible party at least 16 years old who meets all ride requirements ([SDC kids rides guide, via search summary](https://SilverDollarCity.com/news/kids-rides-guide/)). Emma and Tessa (2.5) are almost certainly under 36 inches, so the companion-allowed rides are their only options. Grayson (about 42-44 in) clears the minimum on several family rides. Many family and thrill rides still require an accompanying rider for his height.

### Wristbands and measuring
- Guests are measured at the Measuring Station (Fireman's Landing) or the FireHouse Play Place, and given a color-coded wristband for ride entry ([SDC Parents Guide, via search summary](https://www.silverdollarcity.com/News/Parents-Guide)). Guest Relations can also measure children ([search summary](https://thrillzing.com/theme-parks/silver-dollar-city-height-requirements/)).
- The park says children can ride if they meet both the minimum height and the additional rider requirements set by each ride's manufacturer ([search summary](https://thrillzing.com/theme-parks/silver-dollar-city-height-requirements/)).

### Inferences
- Emma (1.5) and Tessa (2.5): likely under 36 inches. Their only rides are those with an explicit "under 36 in may ride with a supervising companion" rule (Happy Frogs, Up The Ladder, Fire Wagon Frenzy, Lucky's Dizzy Dogs), plus the Carousel (under 42 in with companion). The 36-in-minimum rides with a stated maximum (Ladybugs, Fireman's Flyers) have no companion rule in my sources, so the girls probably cannot ride them unless they measure 36 in or more.
- No source says a 1.5-year-old may ride on a lap or in arms. The "supervising companion" language implies a separate seat. Confirm with ride operators.
- Grayson at 42 to 44 in: should clear the Grand Exposition Coaster (42 in minimum), Happy Frogs, Up The Ladder, Fire Wagon Frenzy, Lucky's Dizzy Dogs, Ladybugs and Fireman's Flyers alone. With a 16+ companion he can ride PowderKeg (42 in companion), Fire In The Hole (36 in companion), Mystic River Falls (40 in companion, per the listing), the Flooded Mine and the Frisco train (under 48 in with companion), and the Carousel (at 42 in or more, no companion needed). He cannot ride Outlaw Run, FireFall, Giant Barn Swing, Time Traveler (51) or WildFire (52). If he measures just under 42 in, the Grand Exposition Coaster needs a companion at 36 in or more.

### Gaps
- Official height chart itself was not reachable. Every number is a search-summary quote.
- Flooded Mine at 48 in with an unspecified companion cutoff, and the Frisco train at 48 in, look high. Verify both.
- Heights for Rampage, Thunderation, Rivertown Landing rides, Half Dollar Holler / Fireman's Landing play areas, and other kiddie rides were not found.
- Whether any ride allows lap-sitting or in-arms riding for under-36-in toddlers: not found in any source.
- Ride length (seconds or minutes) for individual rides: not found.

## 2. Big coasters and thrill rides: status and fall 2026 closures

### Takeaway
All major coasters are listed as operating in the 2026 season (March 13, 2026 to January 2, 2027). The one notable change is that Thunderation closes permanently after the 2026 season, with a final operating day of Jan. 2, 2027. I found no report of a refurbishment closure of the major coasters in October 2026, but I could not see the park's live ride-status page.

### Cited Findings
- Thunderation will close permanently after the 2026 season, announced Feb. 16, 2026, final operating day Jan. 2, 2027, after 33 years. The park cited Marvel Cave Mining Company lore, and nothing about its replacement was disclosed. The 2026 season opened March 13 — [Amusement Today](https://amusementtoday.com/2026/02/after-33-years-silver-dollar-citys-thunderation-to-close/); [Attractions Magazine](https://attractionsmagazine.com/thunderation-roller-coaster-closing-silver-dollar-city/); [Islands.com](https://www.islands.com/2229215/silver-dollar-city-missouri-nostalgic-amusement-park-permanently-closing-thunderation-roller-coaster-2027/)
- The park committed $500 million over 10 years, announced October 2024 — [Attractions Magazine](https://attractionsmagazine.com/thunderation-roller-coaster-closing-silver-dollar-city/)
- No refurbishment or other closures tied to Thunderation's final season were reported — [search summary of Amusement Today / Attractions Magazine](https://amusementtoday.com/2026/02/after-33-years-silver-dollar-citys-thunderation-to-close/)
- Fire In The Hole exists as a 2024 rebuilt coaster (Wikipedia has separate 1972 and 2024 articles) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_in_the_Hole_(2024_roller_coaster))
- Grand Exposition Coaster is a Zamperla family coaster in The Grand Exposition — [Theme Park Insider](https://www.themeparkinsider.com/reviews/silver-dollar-city/)
- Heights for the thrill rides are in the table in Section 1: Time Traveler 51 in, WildFire 52 in, Outlaw Run 48 in, PowderKeg 48 in, Giant Barn Swing 48 in, FireFall 48 in, Mystic River Falls 55 to 77 in.
- Fall hours: Harvest Festival runs Sept. 11 to Oct. 31, 2026, included with regular admission. Operating days and hours vary through the fall, so check the park calendar — [SDC press release](https://prodcms.silverdollarcity.com/press/harvest-festival-2026/); [Table Rock Lake listing](https://business.visittablerocklake.com/events/details/silver-dollar-city-harvest-festival-14340)

### Inferences
- Thunderation is still operating in October 2026 (its last day is Jan. 2, 2027) unless the park announces a temporary closure. This is an inference from the season dates.
- Thrill level (my own judgment, not a published rating): Time Traveler, WildFire, Outlaw Run, FireFall, American Plunge, Giant Barn Swing, Electro Spin and High-Low Silos are high-thrill. PowderKeg, Fire In The Hole and Mystic River Falls are moderate to high. The Grand Exposition Coaster is a family coaster.

### Gaps
- I found no October 2026 ride-by-ride closure list, and no live status for any coaster. Day-of status must come from the SDC app or website.
- Rampage and Thunderation heights, and ride lengths for all coasters, were not found.
- Thrill ratings are not published in the sources I saw.

## 3. Attractions good for ages 0-5

### Takeaway
The park runs a cluster of toddler-friendly rides in Fireman's Landing and The Grand Exposition, plus Half Dollar Holler (a shaded area for preschool and early-elementary kids) and the FireHouse play area. Most small rides start at a 36-inch minimum but let shorter children ride with a companion.

### Cited Findings
- The park's kids rides guide describes the Magnificent Wave Carousel as an all-around favorite for all ages. Guests under 42 in may ride with a supervising companion — [SDC kids rides guide](https://SilverDollarCity.com/news/kids-rides-guide/)
- Happy Frogs, Ladybugs, Fireman's Flyers and Lucky's Dizzy Dogs are described as suited to younger children. Lucky's Dizzy Dogs is called especially suited to toddlers, with the companion rule for under 36 in — [Tripster](https://www.tripster.com/travelguide/luckys-dizzy-dogs/); [Tripster Happy Frogs](https://www.tripster.com/travelguide/happy-frogs/)
- Half Dollar Holler is a shaded area for preschool and early-elementary kids — [Tripster](https://www.tripster.com/travelguide/half-dollar-holler/)
- Strollers: the park's Parents Guide recommends bringing your own stroller for an infant. Folded strollers can ride the trams and buses. Rentals are first-come, first-served unless reserved. The official guide lists single rentals at $23/day and doubles at $27 (a third party shows $24 and $28). Wagons are allowed but may not fit on trams or buses. Lockers cost $12/day — [SDC Parents Guide, via search summary](https://www.silverdollarcity.com/News/Parents-Guide)
- A Preschool Season Passport exists — [SDC](https://www.silverdollarcity.com/tickets/preschool-season-pass/)
- One listing says children 3 and younger get free admission; unverified — [search summary](https://www.silverdollarcity.com/News/Parents-Guide)
- A kid-focused ride guide exists at [Ozarkly Rides Guide, Kids Edition](https://www.ozarkly.com/stories/rides-guide-kids-edition/), but I could not read it.

### Inferences
- A sensible toddler plan for Emma and Tessa is the Carousel, Happy Frogs, Up The Ladder, Fire Wagon Frenzy and Lucky's Dizzy Dogs, each with a 16+ companion. Grayson can join most of these on his own.
- If one adult must stay behind with a stroller and a sleeping toddler, the shaded play areas (Half Dollar Holler, FireHouse) are low-height-risk options.

### Gaps
- Ride-specific stroller parking: not confirmed in any source. Strollers generally go folded on trams and into shops.
- Kiddie-ride areas called "Rivertown Landing" or "Grand Exposition kid rides" were not individually sourced, apart from Happy Frogs, Ladybugs and the Grand Exposition Coaster. No source listed a Rivertown Landing ride list.
- Ride length and per-ride stroller parking are not published in anything I could see.

## 4. Rider Switch / Child Swap and paid skip-the-line

### Takeaway
SDC appears to offer a parent/rider swap, but all evidence is old, unofficial forum and review content, and one account says nobody can ride the same ride twice. For skip-the-line, the park sells a Trailblazer Pass (third-party source only).

### Cited Findings
- Visitors describe a parent swap: tell the greeter, one parent waits with the child while the other rides, then they trade — [Tripadvisor review "Checkout the Parent Swap for rides!!"](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g44160-d103201-r298820526-Silver_Dollar_City-Branson_Missouri.html); [Tripadvisor Branson Forum](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g44160-i200-k7635714-Silver_Dollar_City_rider_switch_with_a_baby_And_more-Branson_Missouri.html)
- One fan forum post says you can time the swap yourself on some rides, but at certain rides (Lost River and AP) you must tell ride operators — [SDC Fans forum](https://sdcfans.com/forums/index.php?topic=1500.0)
- A visitor account and a travel blog say the park does not allow anyone to ride the same attraction twice, so a parent who already rode cannot swap back in — [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowTopic-g44160-i200-k7635714-Silver_Dollar_City_rider_switch_with_a_baby_And_more-Branson_Missouri.html); [Kara Abbey blog](https://karaabbey.com/five-things-to-know-before-visiting-silver-dollar-city-in-branson-mo-2/)
- Trailblazer Pass: a standard tier covers up to 8 skip-the-line rides per visit, starting at $53, and a Super tier gives unlimited skips on select rides, starting at $85. Admission is separate. Listed rides include American Plunge, PowderKeg, FireFall and Fire In The Hole. Mystic River Falls and Fire In The Hole allow only one skip per visit, even on the Super tier — [Tripster](https://www.tripster.com/travelguide/avoid-the-lines-with-a-silver-dollar-city-trailblazer-pass/). Third-party and undated, so confirm the 2026 price and ride list.
- Parking: general is included with admission, Preferred $22, Premier $67 — [Tripster](https://www.tripster.com/travelguide/silver-dollar-city-tips.md)

### Inferences
- For a group with two non-riding toddlers, a swap is most useful on rides with a 48 in minimum (PowderKeg, Outlaw Run, FireFall). With no re-ride allowed, each adult gets one turn per ride, so the group should plan pairs of adults accordingly. Treat this as unconfirmed.
- A Trailblazer Pass is likely not worth buying for the toddlers, since the rides it covers have minimums of 48 in or more (inference).

### Gaps
- No official 2026 rider-switch policy page was found. Evidence is 4 to 15 years old.
- Official Trailblazer Pass pricing and eligibility for fall 2026 were not verified.
- Which specific rides allow a swap: not found.

## 5. New rides announced or opened for 2025-2026

### Takeaway
No new major ride was confirmed to have opened in 2026. The park announced a new family coaster, Miner's Mountain Express, for 2027, and Thunderation closes after 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Miner's Mountain Express: a MACK Rides family coaster announced August 14, 2026 for a 2027 opening, billed as "America's Most Legendary Family Coaster", with fifteen moments of weightlessness and described as the longest family coaster in the Midwest — [Attractions Magazine](https://attractionsmagazine.com/silver-dollar-city-announces-new-mack-ride-2027/); [KY3, Aug. 14, 2026](https://www.ky3.com/video/2026/08/14/silver-dollar-city-reveals-its-next-big-ride/)
- Search results claiming a 2026 ride called "Terra Firma" came from low-quality sites with generic text, and I discounted them — [search results](https://dev1.edwiser.org/tag/silver-dollar-city)
- Context: the park announced a $500 million, 10-year investment in October 2024 — [Attractions Magazine](https://attractionsmagazine.com/thunderation-roller-coaster-closing-silver-dollar-city/)
- Fire In The Hole was rebuilt as a 2024 coaster — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_in_the_Hole_(2024_roller_coaster))

### Inferences
- Miner's Mountain Express is not open in fall 2026. It is a 2027 ride, so it does not affect this trip. Its likely appeal for a family with young children is a candidate for a future trip, though no height requirement has been published.

### Gaps
- Heights for Miner's Mountain Express: not published in the sources seen.
- Whether any 2025 ride opened other than the Fire In The Hole (2024) rebuild: not confirmed.
- Whether the Miner's Mountain Express announcement coincides with Thunderation's removal site: not stated.
